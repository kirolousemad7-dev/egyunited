About

EGY UNITED FOR ELEVATORS — Integrated solutions built around your project

An Egyptian company providing elevator supply and installation, modernization and replacement, fault repair, and monthly maintenance.

We serve residential buildings, villas, companies, malls, hospitals, and industrial and administrative facilities, and we consider the building and the project requirements when defining the right solution.

Service covers every governorate in Egypt, from defining the need and the site visit through execution and follow-up.

  • Supply and installation suited to the building.
  • Modernization of existing elevators and renewal of their components.
  • Repair of faults and technical problems.
  • Monthly maintenance to follow the elevator’s technical condition.
  • Coverage across all governorates of Egypt.
